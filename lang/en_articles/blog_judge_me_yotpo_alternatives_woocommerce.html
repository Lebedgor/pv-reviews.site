💸 The three hidden costs of cloud review platforms

💳 1. The subscription never ends

Cloud review platforms price per order volume. As your store grows, so does the bill — and the features that actually drive conversion (video reviews, custom branding, advanced integrations) are placed in the higher tiers. A store paying $50–$150/month spends $1,800–$5,400 over three years on a component that adds a review form to product pages.

🔒 2. You don't own your social proof

With a cloud platform, your reviews — text, customer photos, videos, ratings history — live on the provider's servers, rendered through their widgets and scripts. If you cancel, downgrade, or the provider changes terms, your review content stops displaying. Years of accumulated trust can vanish from your product pages with a single billing decision. Migrating that content back out is usually possible in theory and painful in practice.

🐌 3. Every product page pays a performance tax

Cloud review widgets inject external JavaScript bundles, iframes, and tracking pixels into every product page you have. That's third-party code you don't control, loading on your most important commercial pages — and it's a common cause of degraded Core Web Vitals scores.
