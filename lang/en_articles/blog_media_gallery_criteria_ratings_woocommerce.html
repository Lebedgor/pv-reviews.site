WooCommerce Review Media Gallery & Custom Rating Criteria

Published on September 10, 2026 · 6 min read

🖼️ Media gallery: two modal windows, smooth browsing

The plugin includes a full-featured media gallery that opens in a modal window:

  1. Thumbnail window — a grid of all photos and videos from reviews (contextual: for a product, or across the all-reviews page).
  2. Full-size viewer — navigation between media, review information, and a custom in-modal video player with its own controls, distinct from the native fullscreen player.

🧮 Custom rating criteria: breakdown, not just one number

Beyond the overall star rating, PVR Pro adds custom rating criteria to WooCommerce product reviews — Quality, Value, Shipping Speed, Durability, or anything you define:

  • Define criteria globally or per product, with a per-criterion rating bar and average.
  • Multilingual titles — criteria names are stored per language (WPML / Polylang / locale prefixes), matching your WordPress locale setup.
  • Bind criteria to product categories with a custom sort order, so electronics stores ask about battery life and clothing stores ask about fit.
  • The summary block renders above the review list, so buyers see strengths and weaknesses at a glance.

💡 Why this matters

A single "4.5 stars" number hides the story. A criteria breakdown — per WooCommerce review, per product — tells buyers exactly where the product is strong. And richer structured content is good for search engines too.
