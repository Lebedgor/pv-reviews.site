🎁 Coupons for reviews: rewards that feed the flywheel

PVR Pro incentivizes customers to leave WooCommerce reviews — and specifically reviews with photos and videos:

  • Automatic WooCommerce coupon generation when a review is approved — different amounts for plain text, with photo, and with video reviews.
  • Coupon restrictions: product/category include & exclude lists, minimum order amount, expiry in days, exclude sale items.
  • Designed coupon email — store logo, your accent colors, a dashed coupon ticket with code, validity, and a linked product card.
  • Reward banner on the all-reviews page: "leave a review, get X" — visible before the review form is even opened.
  • Admin control: issue, resend, or delete the coupon right from the WordPress review editor, plus a test email preview.

💡 Why this matters

Without incentives, only a handful of customers leave reviews. With separate rewards for photos and videos, the user-generated content flow grows significantly. More reviews with media means more trust from new shoppers, broader semantics, and better search rankings for your WooCommerce store.

📧 Review reminders: automated follow-up emails

The plugin automatically emails customers asking for a review when a WooCommerce order reaches the status you choose (for example, "Delivered") — with a configurable delay in days, so the customer has time to actually use the product.

  • Two reminder emails: an initial reminder and a follow-up, each with configurable subject, heading, and message templates.
  • Coupon placeholders inside reminder templates ({coupon_amount_text}, {coupon_amount_photo}, {coupon_amount_video}) — the email itself mentions the reward.
  • Repeat campaigns — a second reminder after a configurable delay, with category exclusions and retry limits.
  • Statistics — queue size, sent, opened, clicked, in the Reminders tab.
  • One-click unsubscribe — every reminder carries a signed unsubscribe link, so automated follow-ups stay polite and spam-compliant.

📬 More reviews without manual effort

Most customers don't mind leaving a review — they just forget. A well-timed email reminds them while impressions are fresh. Reminder + coupon incentive = a noticeable increase in WooCommerce review flow, which means more content for SEO and more trust from new shoppers.

📬 Branded email notifications for every event

Every step of the review lifecycle is covered by styled email notifications — all wrapped in one shared template with your store logo, gradient header, and accent colors. The store owner gets an instant notification for every new review: rating strip, verified-buyer badge, author details, pros & cons, and the attached photo & video grid — with a one-click link to moderation. The customer gets a confirmation the moment their review goes live.
