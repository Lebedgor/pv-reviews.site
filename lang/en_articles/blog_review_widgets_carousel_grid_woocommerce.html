Carousel & Grid Review Widgets for WordPress: Social Proof on Any Page

Published on September 10, 2026 · 5 min read

Standalone WordPress widgets display your best WooCommerce reviews outside the product page — on the homepage, in categories, on landing pages:

  • Carousel — fully responsive: slide count, arrows, and pagination are configured separately for five breakpoints (desktop, two tablet variants, and two mobile variants).
  • Autoplay with configurable delay, inertial swipe, dot or scrollbar pagination — per breakpoint.
  • Grid — the same breakpoint control in a static layout.
  • Reviews can be filtered by media presence (photo/video only), category, or product. Shortcode or PHP call — fits any theme.

💡 Where to use the carousel

  • Homepage — top reviews with photos/videos: instant proof that your WooCommerce store is trusted.
  • Category pages — reviews for products in the category help with decision-making.
  • Landing pages — social proof right next to your offer.
