Every Feature & Setting

A complete guide to every feature in PVR Media Reviews — from photo and video reviews to voting, replies, and schema markup.

Free Plugin

Core Features

Everything included in the free PVR Media Reviews plugin — available for free on the WordPress repository.

Photo Reviews

Customers upload real product photos directly in the review form. Multiple photos per review. Thumbnails with lightbox preview on the product page.

Video Reviews

Customers record or upload videos. MP4 and WebM formats supported. Videos play inline in a modal — no page reloads, no external links.

Client-Side Video Compression

Large videos are compressed in the browser before upload using MediaRecorder API + Canvas. No FFmpeg, no server CPU load. Configurable compression threshold and quality.

Helpful Voting

Visitors can mark reviews as "helpful". The most helpful reviews rise to the top, helping potential buyers make decisions faster.

Store Replies

Store owners can reply to any review directly from the WordPress admin. Replies are displayed publicly below the review.

Reviewer Avatars

Each reviewer can upload a profile photo. Avatars appear next to their reviews, adding visual trust and personalization.

Schema Markup

Automatic JSON-LD structured data for product reviews. Helps Google display star ratings and review snippets in search results.

Email Notifications

Automatic email to store owner when a new review is submitted. Configurable in WooCommerce email settings.

Video Thumbnails

Automatic thumbnail generation from video first frame. Displayed in the gallery grid before the user clicks to play.

Honeypot & Anti-Spam

Built-in invisible honeypot field protection and per-IP rate limiting to block automated bot submissions without frustrating human buyers. Optional Google reCAPTCHA v3 support.

7 Languages Out of the Box

Fully localized with bundled translation JSON files for English, Spanish, French, German, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, and Russian.

Pro Features

Advanced Tools

Everything in Free, plus SEO review pages, analytics, marketing automation, and flexible display widgets.

SEO Review Pages

A dedicated, filterable all-reviews page for your store. Each category filter gets its own auto-generated SEO title, meta description, and URL slug.

Custom Criteria Ratings

Define custom rating criteria per product or globally — Quality, Value, Durability, etc. Each criterion gets its own rating bar and average score.

Email Reminders

Automatically send follow-up emails asking customers to leave a review after purchase. Configurable delay, subject line, and email template.

Coupons for Reviews

Automatically generate discount coupons when a customer leaves a review. Configure discount type (percentage or fixed), amount, and coupon validity period.

Analytics Dashboard

Track review submissions, average ratings, and media uploads over time. Visual charts showing trends and review volume growth.

Carousel & Grid Widgets

Display reviews anywhere — product pages, homepage, landing pages — as a swipeable carousel or responsive grid. Use shortcodes or PHP functions.
